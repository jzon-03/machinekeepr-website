Use Cases

MachineKeepr helps machine shops connect people, processes, and production data across the shop floor.

Job & Production Management

Give operators and supervisors a clear view of active jobs, operations, priorities, and production status from the shop floor.

Machine Scheduling

Organize job sequences around your machines so teams can see what is scheduled, what is running, and what is coming next.

Setup & Inspection

Keep setup instructions, inspection requirements, measurements, and supporting documents available where the work is being performed.

Shop Floor Data Collection

Capture production activity and operator data at the machine, providing real-time visibility into what is happening on the shop floor.

Quality Management

Record inspection results and supporting evidence as work moves through each operation, creating a more complete production record.

Multi-Machine Visibility

Give supervisors and managers a centralized view of jobs, machines, operations, and production activity across the entire shop.
